Cards, cash & staying connected.

At the terminal

Choose Vietnamese dong

If offered INR or VND, compare the disclosed conversion and choose VND to decline the merchant’s conversion. Visa notes that home-currency conversion can include fees and its own exchange rate.

Visa conversion guide ↗
A reliable backup

Carry some local cash

Small vendors may need cash. Consider a modest initial withdrawal using an internationally enabled debit card; check issuer and ATM fees. Credit card withdrawals may incur cash-advance fees and immediate interest. Do not assume Indian UPI or Vietnamese QR works for tourists.

Vietnam payment guide ↗
Phone & OTP

Keep your Indian SIM active

Confirm with your operator that incoming OTP SMS works in Vietnam and what roaming activation or recharge is needed. A data-only eSIM typically cannot receive ordinary SMS. Test eSIM compatibility and install as instructed.

ATM screen

Check fees first

Decline an unwanted home-currency conversion, review ATM charges on screen, and compare rates. Avoid calculating a guaranteed cash amount using a mid-market rate.

Backup plan

Separate cards & emergency cash

Carry an alternate enabled card separately, record bank blocking numbers, and keep some cash in a different place. Retain receipts and transaction alerts.

When plans change

Answers for the unexpected.

Visa is delayed or incorrect?

Check each official application status and registration code; contact Vietnamese immigration or the embassy about corrections. Do not assume approval; compare visa name, passport and dates before travel.

Flight changes or is delayed?

Check the airline directly, notify the host and driver, and ask the airline about your fare’s options. Save delay evidence and check insurance coverage. Confirm the return schedule again before departure.

Host or driver is unreachable?

Use in-app messages and the booking’s contact details; contact Airbnb support if needed. Have the property address offline and an alternate licensed taxi option. Agree an early check-in contingency with the host.

Cruise cancelled or luggage rejected?

Ask for written weather cancellation/refund terms and a transfer alternative before paying. If sailing is cancelled, arrange a replacement night and Ninh Binh transport. Confirm both vehicles and boat accept your exact bags before departure.

Card or phone stops working?

Use the backup card and cash, contact the bank to unblock or replace the card, and use downloaded maps and booking copies. Arrange connectivity via your provider or a local network store.

Passport lost or medical help needed?

Report passport loss to local police, obtain a report and contact the Indian Embassy in Hanoi. For illness, contact your insurer’s emergency assistance and seek local care; keep receipts.

Embassy contacts and passport guidance ↗

Airport driver is late?

Confirm pickup, car, contact and a backup driver the night before; call immediately if late. For less flight-day uncertainty, spend 7 January near Noi Bai rather than Ninh Binh.

Rain or a tiring day?

Swap viewpoints and cycling for a café, spa or rest day. Confirm operating hours, rain closures and activity cancellation terms; notify cruise operator of dietary needs in advance.
